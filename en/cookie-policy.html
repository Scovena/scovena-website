Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar browser-storage technologies used on the SCOVENA website.

1. Technologies currently used

This version of the SCOVENA website does not use analytics, advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. To avoid repeatedly asking for your cookie preference, the following value is stored in your browser's localStorage:

  • Key: scovenaCookieConsent
  • Purpose: Remembering your cookie/similar-technology preference.
  • Party: First-party (SCOVENA website).
  • Duration: Until you clear your browser's site data or reset your preference using "Cookie Settings".

2. Optional technologies

Google Analytics, Meta Pixel and similar advertising tools are not currently enabled. If non-essential analytics, functionality or marketing technologies are added in the future, they will be disabled by default where consent is required and will not be activated without your active choice.

3. Changing your preference

You can reset your stored preference using the Cookie Settings link in the website footer and choose again. You can also delete site data through your browser settings.

4. Hosting and technical logs

The hosting infrastructure may process technical log data such as IP address, date/time and request information for security, troubleshooting and delivery of the website. Such logs are distinct from cookies and may be subject to the hosting provider's technical processes.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at info@scovena.com.
